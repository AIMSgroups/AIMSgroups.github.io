What we work on

Research Projects

AI Safety

Trustworthy AI

Robust and secure AI systems, with emphasis on adversarial machine learning, generative AI safety, privacy, and dependable deployment.

Responsible AI

Advancing Fairness and Bias in AI Systems

Methods for understanding, measuring, and improving fairness across machine-learning and multimodal AI systems.

Identity

Biometrics

Secure and privacy-aware biometric recognition using physiological, behavioral, face, voice, and multimodal signals.

Hardware

Hardware Security and Trust

Trusted hardware, PUFs, roots of trust, secure architectures, and resilience against physical and side-channel attacks.
